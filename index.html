03 / WATER

The approach band drains as you arrive.

The band starts full. Scroll brings the water down. At the bottom the button reads Free Consultation and the border lights. Scroll back up and the water returns with you. Reduced motion skips the effect.

Navy and water

Deep water, accent water, and a pale highlight. Buttons stay dark enough for white type.

Scroll-linked

The fill tracks the band in the viewport. Past the band it stays drained. It is CSS, not a one-shot clip.

Lab check
